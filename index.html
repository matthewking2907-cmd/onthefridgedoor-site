Everything that used to go on the fridge door, now on everyone’s phone.

Meals and the shopping list, chores and stars, plans, reminders and birthdays, shared across your family’s devices.

Start for free How it works

Free while in early access. Works on phones, tablets and computers, even offline.

🍽️

Meals that write the list

Plan four weeks of dinners from 140 family recipes or your own. The shopping list builds itself, grouped by aisle and scaled to your household.

🧹

Chores that suit each child

A chore chart that knows ages, takes turns fairly and gives stars towards rewards. Babies get none, of course.

🗓️

One family calendar

Plans, tasks and bin day in one place, with your Google, Outlook or iCloud calendars alongside. A morning summary and an evening heads-up.

🎂

Birthdays and celebrations

Reminders to buy the present and post the card in time, and gift ideas per person. Choose the celebrations your family marks.

🧻

Never run out

Tap when the toilet roll is low and it goes on the next shop. Suggestions for babies and children as they grow.

📣

Nudges

Send “Dinner’s ready” or “Time for bed” to a family member’s device, and they can reply with one tap.

Your family’s data stays your family’s

Plans live on your own devices. Switch on sync and a private copy is shared only with your household. No adverts, no tracking, nothing sold. Children don’t have accounts: parents choose which devices the app is on, and we only ever ask for a child’s birth month and year.
