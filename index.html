How it shares the load

🙋

Everyone has their own day

Today shows each person their plans, chores and dinner, so nobody needs to ask. Children see theirs on their own phone or tablet, set up without an email.

🍽️

Dinner, already decided

A four-week meal plan from 140 family recipes or your own. The shopping list writes itself, grouped by aisle, so anyone can do the shop.

🧹

Chores that hand themselves out

Shared fairly, taking turns, matched to each child’s age, with stars towards rewards. No more deciding who does what.

🔔

Reminders reach the right person

A morning summary, an evening heads-up and a nudge before each plan, on each person’s own phone. Send “Dinner’s ready” and get a one-tap reply.

🎂

Birthdays nobody forgets

Reminders to buy the present and post the card in time, gift ideas for each person, and the celebrations your family marks.

🧻

Running low? One tap

Anyone can put toilet roll or nappies on the next shop. Nothing to remember, nothing to write down.

📨

Bring everyone in

Invite another parent with a link, and connect a child’s tablet in a minute. Works for any household: two parents, one parent, or grandparents helping out.

Your family’s data stays your family’s

Plans live on your own devices. Switch on sync and a private copy is shared only with your household. No adverts, no tracking, nothing sold. Children don’t have accounts: parents choose which devices the app is on, and we only ever ask for a child’s birth month and year.
