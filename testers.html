On the FridgeDoor is a family planner built around one idea: no one carries it all. Meals, the shopping list, chores, plans and birthdays live in one place that the whole household can see, so it isn’t all in one person’s head.

It’s early, and you’re one of the first families to use it. Here’s what would help most:

Use it for two weeks, as a household. Get the other adults on it if you can, and children too if they’re old enough. Then tell us what worked, what confused you and what you wish it did. There’s a “Send feedback” button in Settings, and we read every message.

Get started (about 5 minutes)

  1. Open the app on your phone

    Go to app.onthefridgedoor.com. It runs in your browser, so there’s nothing to download from a store yet.

  2. Put it on your home screen

    iPhone: open it in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
    Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

    From then on, open it from the icon, not the browser. On iPhone the two keep separate copies.

  3. Answer the setup questions

    Who’s in your family, what you’d like help with (meals, shopping, chores, tasks) and a few details like your shop day. It builds a starter meal plan, shopping list and chore chart from your answers, and you can change everything afterwards.

  4. Switch on sync

    Type your email and then the code we send you. There are no passwords. Sync is what lets everyone in the household share the plan.

    ⚙️ Settings › Sync across devices
  5. Bring everyone in

    Invite another parent sends a link to your partner or another carer. They sign in with their own email and see the same plan. 📱 Add a child’s device connects a child’s phone or tablet with no email or account.

    ⚙️ Settings › Sync across devices, or 👪 Bring everyone in on Today

Opened an invite link but it went to the browser instead of the app? Open the app from its icon, tap Got a family code? and type the code from the message.

What each part does

☀️ Today and ⏭️ Tomorrow

The day at a glance: plans, dinner with its ingredients, and chores. On a device set to one person, it shows their day.

🍽️ Meal plan

A four-week rotation of dinners. Tap a meal, then a day, to place it. Every meal has a recipe with timers, and quantities scale to your household.

🛒 Shopping list

Built from the meals on your plan, grouped by aisle, for your shop day. Add your own items for this shop or every shop.

🧻 Essentials

The things you keep stocked. Tap what’s running low and it lands on the next shop, with a reminder the evening before if reminders are on.

🧹 Chores

A chore chart that rotates fairly and suits each child’s age. 📋 Upcoming chores shows what’s coming up.

☑️ Tasks

One-off jobs with a due date and an owner. Hand one over with 🤝 and they get an “Over to you” nudge.

🗓️ Calendar

Everyone’s plans in one view, including calendars you connect from Google, Outlook or iCloud.

🎂 Occasions

Birthdays, anniversaries and celebrations, with reminders days ahead and gift ideas. Celebrations are only added if you choose them.

🔔 Reminders and nudges

Each phone chooses its own reminders. Parents can send a quick 📣 Nudge from Today (“Time for bed”), and the person can reply “OK” or “On my way” straight from the notification.

🔒 Parent PIN

Set a PIN, then choose Lock for kids in the menu. Children can still see their day and tick chores, but settings, shopping and editing are hidden.

Sending feedback

In the app: ⚙️ Settings › Send feedback. If you’re signed in to sync, it comes straight to us with your email so we can reply. Otherwise it opens an email to hello@onthefridgedoor.com.

The most useful things to tell us:

  • Anything that confused you, or where you didn’t know what to tap next.
  • Anything that looked wrong, like a quantity, a recipe step or a date. A screenshot helps.
  • What you used most, and what you didn’t use at all.
  • What would make you keep using it after the two weeks.

Good to know

Is it free? Yes, all of it, while we test.

iPhone or Android? Both. On Android we’ll also send you a Google Play link later, so you can install it from the Play Store. If you join that test, please stay in it for at least 14 days, because Google checks this before the app can go public.

Your data. Your plan is saved on your phone. With sync on, a copy is kept on our server in London so your household’s devices can share it. Nobody else can see it, there are no adverts and we never sell your data. You can delete your account at any time in Settings. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Something broke? Close the app and open it again; that usually brings the latest version. If it doesn’t, tell us what you were doing and we’ll sort it.
