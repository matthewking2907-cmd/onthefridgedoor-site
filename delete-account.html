Delete your On the FridgeDoor account

You can delete your account and the data we hold for it at any time, from the app or by email.

In the app (straight away)

  1. Open On the FridgeDoor and go to ⚙️ Settings.
  2. In Sync across devices, tap More.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Without the app

Email hello@onthefridgedoor.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We’ll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What’s deleted

  • Your sign-in (your email address).
  • Your devices’ reminder settings and notification addresses, nudges you sent, your calendar links and any feedback you sent.
  • If you’re the last parent in the household: all of the household’s shared data (meal plan, shopping list, chores, tasks, events, birthdays, gift ideas, family names) and any children’s devices connected to it.

What’s kept, and for how long

  • If another parent is still in the household, the household’s shared plan stays for them. Only your own sign-in and data above are removed.
  • Our encrypted weekly backups expire within 90 days, so deleted data is gone from them within 90 days.
  • What’s saved on your own phone or tablet stays there until you clear it (Settings › Start again) or uninstall the app.

Deleting some data but keeping your account

You can delete anything in the app at any time, switch off reminders, remove connected calendars, or leave the household. Or email us and we’ll delete what you ask.
